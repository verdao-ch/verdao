After reviewing the content carefully, I would like us to make some important changes before we move toward the final version.

The main principle I would like us to follow is authenticity. BAMBOOGO already has a genuine story behind it, so I would prefer that we build the website around that real history rather than some of the technological and impact claims currently used as placeholder/concept content.

VERDAO — The Way to Good.
VERDAO is the wider purpose-driven initiative/organisation.

BAMBOOGO — Ride the Story.
BAMBOOGO is the bamboo bicycle brand/project being developed under VERDAO.

“Ride the Story” is intentional and important to me. The BAMBOOGO project comes from a real personal and professional journey with bicycles and bamboo bicycle building, so I would like storytelling to become a central part of the website.

1. What has already happened / exists today

For now, we can develop the storytelling and crowdfunding information and keep the actual payment functionality separate until the appropriate solution is selected.

2. Our Story

Every Story Has a Beginning
2016 — The Bicycle Journey
Okey Omulu began working professionally with bicycle assembly and maintenance in Switzerland in 2016.
Over the following years, his work expanded across bicycle assembly, maintenance, repair, and later
mechanical and electric bicycles.
2019/2020 — Discovering Bamboo
His interest in bicycles eventually led beyond conventional frame materials. In Basel, Okey learned the
craft of bamboo bicycle construction through Le Bambousier and began building bamboo bicycles himself.
A Le Bambousier post dated 2 January 2020 publicly documented one of his self-built bamboo bicycles.
Building Three Bicycles in Switzerland
Okey went on to build three bamboo bicycles in Switzerland. Building by hand provided first-hand
understanding of the material, the construction process, and the skill, patience and time required to
produce a quality bamboo bicycle.
The Challenge
The experience also revealed practical challenges. Bamboo bicycle construction is labour-intensive, which
can make small-scale production in Switzerland expensive. Sourcing bamboo with the characteristics
required for bicycle-frame construction can also be challenging locally.
Instead of abandoning the idea, these challenges inspired a different question: Could production be
developed in a way that combines suitable bamboo resources, craftsmanship, skills development and
economic opportunity?
2021 — Bamboo Bikes Bern
The idea developed further through Bamboo Bikes Bern, establishing the foundation for what would later
evolve into BAMBOOGO.
The Boogaali Connection
As the project developed, Okey established a relationship with Boogaali, an experienced bamboo bicycle
builder in Uganda. Boogaali subsequently supplied three bamboo bicycle frames for project development.
These frames have been assembled into complete bicycles in Switzerland.
Discussions have also included Boogaali supporting future training in bamboo bicycle construction as the
project develops.
The Next Chapter
The journey that began with learning and building has now evolved into VERDAO and BAMBOOGO.
BAMBOOGO brings that history forward into a new bicycle brand built around craftsmanship, sustainable
materials and the belief that every bicycle can carry a bigger story.
Ride the Story.
Important wording note for development: Do not turn the Boogaali relationship into claims such as “our
Uganda factory,” “our African production partner,” or “BAMBOOGO currently trains people in Africa” unless
those descriptions become formally accurate.

4. BAMBOOGO
Ride the Story.
BAMBOOGO is a bamboo bicycle project born from years of hands-on bicycle experience and bamboo
bicycle building.
We are developing bicycles that bring together the natural character of bamboo with modern bicycle
components, thoughtful design and skilled assembly.
Every BAMBOOGO should carry two stories: the story behind how it was created and the story its rider
creates with it.
Why Bamboo?
Renewable material — Bamboo is a rapidly growing natural material with interesting possibilities for
bicycle-frame construction.
Natural character — No two bamboo stems are exactly identical, giving each handcrafted frame its own
character.
Ride experience — Bamboo is valued by frame builders for material characteristics that can contribute to
a distinctive ride feel.
Craftsmanship — A bamboo frame brings visible craftsmanship into a product category dominated by
industrial manufacturing.
Grown, not forged.

Use as supporting product/campaign language. Avoid unsupported claims such as “17% better damping
than carbon,” “carbon negative,” “100% sustainability,” or “stronger than steel” unless evidence is obtained
for the specific claim.
